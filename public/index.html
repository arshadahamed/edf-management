100% Zakat & Amanah Charity Standard

100% Direct Field Impact. Zero Admin Deduction on Zakat.

Since 2016, EDF Galgamuwa has operated as an accountable Islamic charity anchored in trust (Amanah). Our founding patrons and trustees underwrite core administration costs — so every rupee of your Zakat and direct cause sponsorship reaches education and vulnerable households intact.

Ring-Fenced Zakat & Sadaqah Jariyah

Zakat goes exclusively to verified eligible families; Sadaqah Jariyah powers free schools, Madrasas, and toolkits.

Monetary, Equipment & Repair Tracking

Every grant — cash aid, sewing machines, school packs, or home roof reconstruction — is logged inside beneficiary dossiers.

Home-Verified & Community Audited

Field visits, receipt archives, and transparent AGM financial audits safeguard donor confidence every year.

Charity Transparency & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on how your Zakat, Sadaqah, equipment aid, and sponsorships reach families across Galgamuwa.

How does EDF verify families and distribute Zakat & Sadaqah?
Field coordinators conduct verified home visits and household registry reviews with community elders before approving any aid. Every rupee of Zakat is ring-fenced and disbursed 100% directly to eligible households, with trustees and members covering administrative overheads.
Why are Education and Community Development your main focus?
While emergency food packs relieve immediate hunger, lasting liberation from poverty requires schooling, marketable trades, and stable family income. Over 80% of our charity budget is invested directly in Madrasa & academic education, youth technical skills, women's micro-enterprises, and home repairs.
Can I donate equipment, school kits, or sponsor a specific home repair?
Yes! Alongside Zakat and Sadaqah sponsorships, donors regularly contribute sewing machines for widows, laptops and mobile-repair toolkits for youth trainees, school stationery packs, mobility medical gear, or materials for home roof and shelter reconstruction — all tracked transparently in individual beneficiary logs.
How are Madrasa and academic student programmes managed?
Our dedicated Madrasa & Education wing manages qualified Muallimahs and tutors, daily attendance records, structured classes, free school uniforms, textbooks, and higher-education career mentoring for over 100 local children year-round.
How can I volunteer, partner, or visit the EDF centre in Galgamuwa?
Donors, educators, doctors, technicians, and partner organisations are warmly welcome to visit our center, inspect our programmes, or volunteer skills. Message our Charity Desk via WhatsApp at +94 71 535 8380 ahead of your visit.
